Privacy Policy

Cafe POS · Last updated: 16 November 2026

Cafe POS is a point-of-sale service used by cafes and restaurants ("the cafe") to take orders and make bills. This policy explains what personal data the service handles and how it is protected. It is written with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 in mind.

1. Who is responsible

The cafe decides why customer data is collected (for example, to send you your bill) and is the Data Fiduciary for its customers. Cafe POS (the platform operator) processes that data on the cafe's behalf to run the service.

2. Data we handle

  • Customers of a cafe (optional): name and mobile number, if you give them at the counter, linked to your bills (items, amounts, date and time).
  • Cafe staff: login username, role and the cafe they belong to. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication and are never visible to the cafe or to us.
  • Cafes: business details (name, address, phone, GST number), menu, bills and settings.
  • Activity records: who cancelled or edited a bill or exported data, and when (without passwords).

We do not collect payment card details: payments are only marked as "cash", "UPI" or "card" on the bill.

3. Why it is used

  • To make and print your bill and, if you agree, send it to you on WhatsApp.
  • To show the cafe its own customer list and sales reports.
  • To keep the service secure and to prevent misuse of bills.

Your number is not sold and not used for advertising by Cafe POS. Each cafe sees only its own data.

4. Where it is stored and how it is protected

Data is stored on Google Cloud / Firebase, encrypted in transit and at rest. Each cafe's data is kept separate by server-side security rules; staff can access only their own cafe, and every login can be turned off at once.

5. How long it is kept

Each cafe chooses how long customer names and numbers stay on its bills (12, 24 or 36 months, or as set by the cafe). After that the name and number are removed; the bill amounts stay for the cafe's accounts and tax records, which the law may require to be kept.

6. Your rights

You can ask the cafe to show, correct or delete your name and number. The cafe can remove them from all your bills from its Customers screen. You can also withdraw your consent at any time by asking the cafe not to save your number.

7. Contact

For questions about your data, please contact the cafe where you made your purchase. For questions about the platform, contact the Cafe POS operator at the support email shown by your cafe.
