Cafe POS · Last updated: 16 November 2026

1. The service

Cafe POS provides software for cafes and restaurants to take orders, make bills, manage menus and view reports, on a subscription plan (including any free trial).

2. Accounts and logins

  • The cafe is responsible for its logins and for keeping passwords and the Manager PIN secret.
  • Logins can be turned off at any time by the cafe owner or the platform operator.

3. Bills and records

  • Bills receive sequential numbers per financial year. Bills are cancelled (with a reason), not deleted, so the series stays complete.
  • The cafe is responsible for the prices, taxes (including GST) and details printed on its bills.

4. Subscription, trial and suspension

  • When a trial or subscription ends without renewal, billing is paused. The cafe's data is kept and becomes usable again when the subscription is renewed.
  • The operator may suspend a cafe for non-payment or misuse. Data is not deleted because of suspension.

5. Data

The cafe owns its business data. Personal data is handled as described in the Privacy Policy. On request when a subscription ends, the cafe can receive an export of its data.

6. Availability

The service runs on Google Firebase and is designed to be available at all times, but short interruptions can happen. The app keeps working briefly offline and syncs when the connection returns.

7. Liability

The service is provided as is. The operator's liability is limited to the fees paid for the current subscription period, to the extent permitted by law.
